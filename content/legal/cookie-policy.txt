2. WHAT ARE COOKIES?
Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your browser or device when you visit a website.
They allow a website to recognise a browser or device and retain certain information between visits or during a browsing session.
Depending on their purpose, cookies may:
Maintain account login sessions.
Support website security.
Remember user preferences.
Improve website functionality.
Measure website performance.
Help understand visitor behaviour.
Support advertising and marketing activities, where applicable.
Cookies do not automatically provide access to all information stored on your device.
