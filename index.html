Worth knowing

A clear picture.

Where we are today.
And where we’re heading.

What can I do with SONKOFLOW today?

You can create an account, organize projects, upload photographs to private storage, review them, download WebP copies, and delete them. AI enhancement, generation, Brand Studio, and batch processing are not available yet.

Which images can I upload?

JPEG, PNG, and WebP images up to 10 MB and 25 megapixels, with a minimum size of 64 × 64 pixels. Animated images are not supported. Uploaded photographs are re-encoded as WebP with embedded metadata removed. Each account can store up to 100 images and 250 MB.

Are the showcase images real product results?

No. The portfolio uses original AI-created concept imagery to show our visual direction. It is not customer work or a demonstration of SONKOFLOW processing. The interactive comparison is a clearly labeled browser color study.

Will AI editing preserve every product detail?

Exact preservation cannot be guaranteed with generative editing. Future workflows will require careful review of labels, colors, shape, identity, and other distinctive details. No AI editing is currently offered.

How are my photographs stored?

Photographs are stored in a private bucket with account-scoped access. You can delete your images from the workspace. The owner still needs to publish privacy, retention, and provider data-handling policies. Avoid sensitive photographs until those policies are available.

Can I buy a plan or image credits?

No paid plans, subscriptions, or image credits are currently sold. Pricing, entitlements, batch limits, and cancellation terms will be published when those services are ready.
