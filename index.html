A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Can I upload and enhance photos today?

Not yet. This is the SONKOFLOW marketing site. Uploads, accounts, AI processing, and downloads are not implemented. Artwork and workspace concepts are labeled throughout.

Which image types and sizes will be supported?

Supported file types, upload sizes, and output resolutions have not been finalized. These will be published after the processing pipeline is implemented and tested.

Will my product details stay exactly the same?

Exact preservation cannot be guaranteed with generative editing. The planned workflows will prioritize important details and require review of labels, colors, shape, and other distinctive features.

How will credits and batch limits work?

Credit prices, batch limits, retry policies, and plan entitlements are not configured yet. No purchases or processing charges can be made on this site.

What happens to my private photographs?

This site does not accept image uploads. Before the application launches, we need to publish its privacy policy, storage and retention practices, deletion procedures, and provider data handling disclosures.

Can I download results or cancel a subscription?

There are currently no generated results, subscriptions, or billing accounts. Download formats and cancellation terms will be documented alongside the implemented services.
