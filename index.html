BEFORE YOUR NEXT PHOTO

A few things
worth knowing.

More about plans & usage
What can I do with SonkoFlow?

AI enhancement has three dedicated modes: personal, professional portrait and product. The workspace also supports private uploads, projects, product packs, Brand Studio, bulk orchestration, quality review and a searchable image library. Processing availability depends on activated services and your allowance; purchasing remains unavailable until merchant setup is verified.

Will every detail stay exactly the same?

No. Your photograph guides the edit and product-preservation instructions are prioritized, but generative models can change appearance, labels, colors and fine details. Review the original and result before using an image. Quality checks support your decision; they do not guarantee perfect fidelity.

How do processing units work?

Quality, canvas, reference complexity, pack outputs and optional AI assessment affect usage. Review your estimate and reservation before generating. Uploads, library organization and downloads do not use AI units. Packs and bulk jobs contain separately metered outputs; requested corrections can use more units.

Are my images public?

Images are kept in private storage and served through account-authorized routes. They are not published to a public gallery by this application. When you request AI processing, the necessary reference content is sent to the configured processing provider. Read the Privacy Policy before uploading personal or confidential material.

Can I return to a previous project?

Yes. Projects, saved originals, outputs and recorded job settings remain connected to your account. The image library supports folders, search, archives and a 30-day photo recovery window. Permanent cleanup depends on scheduled maintenance; deleting a photo does not automatically erase associated usage or audit records.
