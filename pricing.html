A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Know where
you stand.

What is included in Free?

A verified-email workspace and, once sample processing is activated, one lifetime Essential square edit within a 20-unit processing budget. No card, no recurring charge, no weekly refill. A reference outside the reviewed cost range cannot use the sample. Shared daily sample limits also apply.

How many images will my units create?

There is no fixed images-per-unit conversion. Output quality, size, reference complexity and optional AI assessment change cost. Review the estimate before generating. A provider can incur usage even if an image is invalid or cannot be saved; those outcomes are shown honestly.

Do packs and bulk jobs use more?

Yes. A pack contains several separately metered outputs, and a bulk job contains several source packs. They draw from the same prepaid balance. Available allowance, storage reservations and shared concurrency limits must all permit the work.

What happens when my allowance runs out?

New work stops when your available balance cannot cover its reservation. You can choose a one-time pack or wait for a verified subscription renewal. We do not automatically buy extra units or bill usage overages. Unexpected provider costs or refunds of already-consumed capacity may create a balance deficit that must be resolved before new work.

Can I buy once without subscribing?

Yes, when merchant checkout is activated. One-time packs do not renew and purchased units do not expire while your account exists. They provide core Essential and Studio processing and product-pack access, not subscription-only Brand Studio generation, Signature processing or bulk orchestration.

What do Essential, Studio and Signature mean?

These are processing settings, not guarantees of a particular result. Essential uses the provider’s low setting, Studio uses medium, and Signature uses high. Higher settings can consume more processing units. Your original photograph and requested changes also affect the result.

How do monthly and annual plans work?

Monthly plans grant units after each verified paid invoice. Where enabled, yearly billing charges $86.40 for Starter or $182.40 for Pro up front and grants the full yearly allowance up front: 1,200 or 3,000 units. That saves 20% compared with twelve monthly charges. Annual plans renew yearly until cancelled; this is one upfront payment per year. Annual checkout activates only when its merchant plans are configured. Unused granted units remain in your balance.

Can I cancel? What happens to my photographs?

You can stop future subscription billing from your billing page. Subscription features remain through the verified paid period; projects and previously granted units are retained. Stored data remains subject to account/storage limits and the documented recovery policy. Trashed photos have a 30-day recovery window and require scheduled maintenance for permanent removal. We do not delete your projects just because a subscription ends.

How are payments and refunds handled?

PayPal is the planned payment provider. Approval or a browser redirect does not unlock an allowance: the server must verify the payment notification. Checkout shows the agreed USD total before you leave. Provider currency-conversion terms may apply. Tax and legal disclosures must be reviewed before merchant activation. A verified refund removes the corresponding purchased units; this does not promise a blanket refund entitlement.

Will every product detail or portrait stay identical?

No generative system can promise perfect fidelity, text, color or detail reproduction. Every edit starts from your original reference. Product preservation controls, quality checks, comparison and your final review help you make a considered decision.
