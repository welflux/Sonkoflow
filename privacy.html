01Introduction

SONKOFLOW ("we", "us", "our" or the "Company") is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy and personal information of individuals who access or use our website, applications and digital services.

SONKOFLOW is operated by AaronPrime PTY LTD, a company registered in the Republic of South Africa.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, disclose and protect personal information when you visit https://sonkoflow.com, create an account, upload images, purchase a subscription, use our AI-powered image-processing services or communicate with us.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable data-protection laws, including the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA), where applicable.

By using SONKOFLOW, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

03Personal information we collect

The categories of information we collect depend on how you interact with SONKOFLOW and which Services you use.

3.1 Account and Registration Information

When you create or manage an Account, we may collect:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Account identification details.
  • Authentication and login-related information.
  • Password-related authentication data, where applicable.
  • Account preferences and settings.
  • Account creation and activity information.

Where third-party authentication is enabled, we may also receive information made available through the relevant authentication provider.

3.2 Uploaded Images and User Content

When you use SONKOFLOW's image-processing Services, you may submit:

  • Original photographs.
  • Product images.
  • Portrait images.
  • Other uploaded image files.
  • Processing instructions and prompts.
  • Related project information.
  • Generated or enhanced image outputs.

Images may contain personal information, including identifiable individuals or other information visible within the image.

You should only upload images that you are authorised to submit and process.

3.3 Image Processing and Usage Information

We may collect information relating to your use of the Services, including:

  • Processing requests.
  • Selected image-enhancement modes.
  • Processing status and job information.
  • Usage allowances and credit consumption.
  • Processing errors and technical logs.
  • Download activity.
  • Project and gallery information.

This information helps us operate the platform, manage usage, investigate technical issues and protect against misuse.

3.4 Payment and Transaction Information

When you purchase a Subscription or Credit Pack, we may process information relating to the transaction, including:

  • Purchase and subscription records.
  • Selected subscription plan.
  • Billing interval.
  • Transaction reference.
  • Payment status.
  • Amount and currency.
  • Subscription renewal and cancellation information.
  • Relevant billing records.

Payments may be handled by third-party payment providers.

Where payment details are collected directly by a payment provider, those details are generally subject to that provider's own privacy and security practices.

We do not intend to store complete payment-card details on SONKOFLOW systems where these are handled directly by the payment provider.

3.5 Technical and Device Information

When you access SONKOFLOW, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating-system information.
  • Access timestamps.
  • Pages and platform features accessed.
  • Session and diagnostic information.
  • Security and error logs.

This information may be used to maintain the security, reliability and performance of the Services.

3.6 Communications and Support Information

If you contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • The contents of your communication.
  • Support requests.
  • Complaint details.
  • Relevant account or transaction references.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when requesting assistance.

We use this information to respond to enquiries and provide customer support.

04How we use personal information

We collect and process personal information for legitimate operational, contractual and legal purposes, including:

4.1.Creating, managing and authenticating user Accounts.

4.2.Providing access to SONKOFLOW's image-enhancement and image-production Services.

4.3.Processing uploaded images and delivering requested outputs.

4.4.Managing projects, image galleries, downloads and saved results.

4.5.Managing subscriptions, Credit Packs, payments and usage allowances.

4.6.Monitoring processing requests and maintaining accurate usage records.

4.7.Providing customer support and responding to enquiries.

4.8.Detecting fraudulent activity, unauthorised access and misuse of the platform.

4.9.Maintaining, troubleshooting and improving the reliability and security of the Services.

4.10.Communicating important service updates, account notices and transaction-related information.

4.11.Complying with applicable legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

4.12.Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims where necessary.

We will not process personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the purpose for which it was originally collected unless an appropriate legal basis exists.

05AI image processing and uploaded content

SONKOFLOW uses artificial intelligence and automated image-processing technologies to enhance and transform images submitted by users.

5.1 Processing of Uploaded Images

When you upload an image, the relevant file and associated processing information may be transmitted to the systems and service providers necessary to complete your requested operation.

This may include third-party AI-processing infrastructure.

5.2 Purpose of Processing

Uploaded images are processed to:

  • Perform the image enhancement requested by the user.
  • Generate requested product image variations.
  • Improve or transform portrait images.
  • Complete applicable image-processing workflows.
  • Deliver the resulting outputs to the user's Account.

5.3 AI Training and Model Improvement

SONKOFLOW does not represent that user-uploaded images are used to train its own AI models.

Where external AI providers are involved, their handling of submitted content is subject to their applicable terms, privacy policies and data-processing arrangements.

We will disclose the relevant processing arrangements accurately and will not promise that uploaded content is excluded from provider-side model improvement or retention unless that exclusion has been verified and is contractually or technically supported.

5.4 Sensitive and Confidential Images

Users should avoid uploading confidential, highly sensitive or personal images unless they have a legitimate reason and are comfortable with the processing required to provide the requested Service.

You are responsible for obtaining any necessary permissions before submitting photographs containing identifiable individuals.

06Legal basis for processing

Where POPIA or other applicable data-protection legislation requires a lawful basis for processing, SONKOFLOW will rely on an appropriate basis depending on the nature and purpose of the processing.

These may include:

  • Consent: Where you voluntarily consent to a particular processing activity.
  • Contractual necessity: Where processing is necessary to provide Services you have requested or to fulfil an agreement with you.
  • Legal obligation: Where processing is required to comply with applicable legislation.
  • Legitimate interests: Where processing is necessary for legitimate operational, security or business interests, provided that applicable legal requirements and individual rights are respected.

Where consent is relied upon, you may withdraw it, subject to applicable legal requirements and the consequences of withdrawing consent for the relevant Service.

Withdrawal of consent does not invalidate processing lawfully undertaken before withdrawal.

07Sharing and disclosure of personal information

SONKOFLOW does not sell personal information to third parties as a commercial data-brokering activity.

However, we may share or disclose personal information where necessary for the operation of the Services or where legally permitted or required.

7.1 Technology and Infrastructure Providers

We may use third-party providers to support:

  • Website and application hosting.
  • Database and file storage.
  • Account authentication.
  • AI image processing.
  • Email delivery and communications.
  • Technical monitoring and security.
  • Payment processing.

These providers may process personal information on our behalf or independently in accordance with their respective roles and applicable contractual arrangements.

7.2 Payment Providers

Where a payment provider processes your transaction, relevant information may be shared to authenticate payments, confirm transactions, manage subscriptions, process refunds and prevent fraud.

7.3 Legal and Regulatory Disclosure

We may disclose personal information where reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable legislation.
  • Respond to lawful requests from competent authorities.
  • Protect the rights, property or security of SONKOFLOW or other individuals.
  • Investigate suspected fraud or unlawful activity.
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

7.4 Business Transfers

If AaronPrime PTY LTD undergoes a lawful restructuring, merger, acquisition or transfer of business assets, personal information may be transferred where legally permitted and subject to appropriate safeguards.

08International data transfers

SONKOFLOW is operated from South Africa and may use third-party technology providers whose infrastructure, processing facilities or support operations are located in other countries.

Consequently, personal information may be transferred to or processed in jurisdictions outside South Africa.

Where such transfers involve personal information protected by POPIA, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the applicable requirements of Section 72 of POPIA are satisfied.

Depending on the circumstances, this may involve appropriate contractual safeguards, adequate protection under the receiving country's laws or another legally permitted transfer mechanism.

09Data retention

SONKOFLOW retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • The type of information collected.
  • Whether your Account remains active.
  • The nature of the Services used.
  • Applicable subscription and transaction records.
  • Security and fraud-prevention requirements.
  • Legal, regulatory and dispute-resolution obligations.

Uploaded images, generated outputs and project information may remain associated with your Account while the relevant storage facilities are available and the applicable retention conditions are satisfied.

We may delete or anonymise information when it is no longer required, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

Where deletion is requested, we will assess the request and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law.

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

10Data security

SONKOFLOW takes the protection of personal information seriously.

We aim to implement reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against:

  • Unauthorised access.
  • Accidental loss.
  • Unlawful processing.
  • Unauthorised alteration.
  • Unauthorised disclosure.
  • Destruction or misuse.

Depending on the relevant systems, safeguards may include access controls, authentication protections, restricted storage access, secure communications, monitoring and other appropriate security measures.

However, no internet-based platform or electronic transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are also responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your Account credentials and notifying us promptly of suspected unauthorised access.

If SONKOFLOW becomes aware of a security compromise involving personal information, we will assess the incident and take reasonable steps to investigate, contain and address it.

Where a security compromise meets the applicable legal notification requirements, we will notify the relevant regulatory authority and affected individuals in accordance with applicable law.
