# SonkoFlow visual identity

Original framed-S mark, created for this redesign. The flowing S suggests continuity from an original photograph to its next composition; the offset corners refer to framing and image review. It replaces the previous generic sparkle symbol.

- `wordmark-ink.svg` / `wordmark-white.svg`: primary lockups for light/dark backgrounds, resolution-independent outlined glyphs.
- `mark-ink.svg` / `mark-white.svg`: compact marks; also used in the favicon and appropriate workspace details.
- `wordmark-ink@2x.png` / `wordmark-white@2x.png`: transparent 2,400-pixel raster exports for reuse outside the website.
- Wordmark letterforms derive from the existing self-hosted Manrope font, weight 750. Its SIL Open Font License remains in `public/fonts/OFL-Manrope.txt`. SVG outlines do not require external fonts or third-party requests.

Keep the mark/wordmark proportions intact, with at least one small-mark stroke width of clear space around it. Use the light asset on a dark ink background, the ink asset on white, warm paper or pale sage. Do not add gradients, badges or an unrelated AI symbol. Product artwork retains its provenance in `public/images/README.md` and is not evidence of a verified image-generation result.
